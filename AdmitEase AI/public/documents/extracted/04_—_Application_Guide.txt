1. Introduction

This document explains the sample undergraduate application process for Westbridge University for the 2027/2028 academic session.

It provides prospective applicants with guidance on programme selection, eligibility checks, document preparation, application fee payment and the next steps after submitting an application.

AdmitEase AI should use this document when answering questions about how to apply to Westbridge University.

Important Notice: Westbridge University is a fictional institution created for this project. All application procedures, dates and fees in this document are sample information developed for demonstration and testing purposes.

2. Application Period

The sample application period for the 2027/2028 academic session is:

Application opening date: 1 March 2027

Application deadline: 30 August 2027

Application fee: ₦25,000

Applicants are encouraged to prepare their documents and review the admission requirements before beginning the application process.

3. Step-by-Step Application Process

Applicants are expected to follow these steps:

Step 1: Choose a Programme

Review the Westbridge University Undergraduate Programme Catalogue and select a preferred programme.

Available programmes include:

Computer Science

Software Engineering

Accounting

Business Administration

Economics

Political Science

Mass Communication

Biochemistry

Applicants should choose a programme that matches their academic interests and qualifications.

Step 2: Check Admission Eligibility

Review the Undergraduate Admission Requirements document.

Applicants should confirm that they:

Meet the sample minimum UTME score of 160.

Have at least five relevant O'Level credit passes.

Have the required programme-specific subjects.

Meet the maximum two-sitting O'Level requirement.

Meeting these requirements does not guarantee admission.

Step 3: Prepare Required Documents

Applicants should prepare the following information and documents:

Recent passport photograph.

UTME registration details or result.

O'Level result (WAEC, NECO, NABTEB or accepted equivalent).

Valid email address.

Active phone number.

Personal information.

Additional programme-specific documents, where applicable.

Applicants should ensure that their information is accurate and that their documents are readable.

Step 4: Complete the Application

Applicants should complete the university's application process using the official application channel when one becomes available.

They should:

Provide accurate personal information.

Enter their UTME details correctly.

Provide their O'Level examination information.

Select their preferred programme.

Review their entries before submission.

Important: This project does not provide a live application portal. AdmitEase AI must not invent an application URL or claim that applicants can submit applications directly through the assistant.

Step 5: Pay the Application Fee

The sample application fee is ₦25,000.

Applicants should follow the university's verified payment instructions when an official payment channel becomes available.

AdmitEase AI must not provide invented bank account details, payment links or transaction instructions.

Step 6: Review and Submit the Application

Before submitting, applicants should:

Confirm that their personal information is correct.

Check their selected programme.

Verify their examination details.

Ensure that all required documents are available.

Confirm that they have followed the official payment instructions.

Step 7: Await Screening and Admission Updates

After completing the application, applicants should monitor the official university communication channel for screening instructions and admission updates.

AdmitEase AI cannot access individual application records or confirm an applicant's admission status.

5. Application Fee and Payment Guidelines

The sample application fee for the 2027/2028 session is ₦25,000.

Important payment guidelines:

The application fee is separate from tuition fees.

Applicants should use only verified university payment instructions.

Applicants should retain evidence of payment.

Applicants should not make payments to unverified individuals or accounts.

The assistant must not request bank details, card details, PINs or passwords.

No actual payment channel is available within this project.

6. After Submitting an Application

After submitting an application through an official channel, applicants should:

Keep their application confirmation or reference details.

Monitor their registered email and phone number for communication.

Follow any screening instructions provided by the university.

Check the official communication channel for admission updates.

Contact the admissions office if they need assistance with their application.

AdmitEase AI cannot retrieve application records, track individual applications or confirm admission decisions.
