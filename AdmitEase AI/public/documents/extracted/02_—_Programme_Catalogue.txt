Undergraduate Programme Catalogue

1. Introduction

Westbridge University offers undergraduate academic programmes across four faculties.

The programmes are designed to provide students with academic knowledge, practical skills and opportunities for intellectual and professional development.

This catalogue provides an overview of the undergraduate programmes available at the university.

2. Faculty of Computing and Technology

2.1 Computer Science

Computer Science focuses on the principles of computing, software development, programming, data management and computational problem-solving.

Students develop an understanding of computer systems, algorithms, programming languages and modern computing technologies.

Key areas of study include:

Programming and Software Development

Data Structures and Algorithms

Database Management

Computer Networks

Artificial Intelligence Fundamentals

Operating Systems

Programme duration: 4 years.

2.2 Software Engineering

Software Engineering focuses on the design, development, testing and maintenance of software applications and systems.

Students learn how to apply engineering principles to software development and manage software projects effectively.

Key areas of study include:

Software Design and Architecture

Programming and Application Development

Software Testing and Quality Assurance

Web and Mobile Application Development

Software Project Management

Systems Analysis

Programme duration: 4 years.

3. Faculty of Management and Social Sciences

3.1 Accounting

Accounting focuses on financial information, reporting, auditing, taxation and financial management.

Students develop the knowledge and skills required to understand, prepare and interpret financial records.

Key areas of study include:

Financial Accounting

Management Accounting

Auditing

Taxation

Financial Reporting

Business Finance

Programme duration: 4 years.

3.2 Business Administration

Business Administration provides an understanding of how organisations operate, manage resources and achieve business objectives.

Students explore management principles, entrepreneurship, organisational behaviour and business strategy.

Key areas of study include:

Principles of Management

Human Resource Management

Marketing

Business Communication

Entrepreneurship

Strategic Management

Programme duration: 4 years.

3.3 Economics

Economics examines how individuals, businesses and governments make decisions about resources, production, distribution and consumption.

Students explore economic systems, markets, public policy and financial decision-making.

Key areas of study include:

Microeconomics

Macroeconomics

Development Economics

Monetary Economics

Public Finance

Econometrics

Programme duration: 4 years.

3.4 Political Science

Political Science examines political institutions, government systems, public policies and relationships between individuals, societies and political organisations.

Students develop an understanding of governance, political behaviour and international relations.

Key areas of study include:

Political Theory

Nigerian Government and Politics

Comparative Politics

Public Administration

International Relations

Public Policy

Programme duration: 4 years.

4. Faculty of Communication and Humanities

4.1 Mass Communication

Mass Communication focuses on the creation, production and distribution of information through different media channels.

Students explore journalism, broadcasting, public relations, digital media and communication practices.

Key areas of study include:

Journalism

Broadcast Media

Public Relations

Advertising

Digital Media Communication

Media Writing and Production

Programme duration: 4 years.

5. Faculty of Science

5.1 Biochemistry

Biochemistry explores the chemical processes and biological molecules that support life.

Students study the relationship between biology and chemistry, with applications in health, research and biotechnology.

Key areas of study include:

Molecular Biology

Cell Biology

Enzymology

Metabolism

Medical Biochemistry

Biotechnology Fundamentals

Programme duration: 4 years.
