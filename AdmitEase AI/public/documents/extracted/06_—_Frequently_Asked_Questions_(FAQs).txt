1. Introduction

This document contains frequently asked questions and approved answers relating to Westbridge University and its undergraduate admission process.

It is designed to help AdmitEase AI respond to common enquiries from prospective students, parents and guardians.

The assistant should use these answers as a reference while maintaining a natural, helpful and conversational tone.

Important Notice: Westbridge University is a fictional institution created for this project. All institutional details, programmes, admission information, fees and dates are sample information developed for demonstration and testing purposes.

3. Admission Requirements

Q7. What is the minimum UTME score required for admission?

The sample minimum UTME score for the 2027/2028 admission session is 160. Applicants must also satisfy the subject requirements for their chosen programme.

Q8. What are the general O'Level requirements?

Applicants must have at least five relevant credit passes, including English Language. Mathematics and other required subjects depend on the selected programme.

Q9. Can I apply with two O'Level sittings?

Yes. The sample admission policy accepts O'Level results from a maximum of two sittings.

Q10. Is English Language compulsory for all programmes?

Yes. English Language is compulsory for all undergraduate programmes in the sample admission requirements.

Q11. Are admission requirements the same for every programme?

No. Each programme has specific O'Level and UTME subject combinations. Applicants should consult the Undergraduate Admission Requirements document.

Q12. Does meeting the minimum UTME score guarantee admission?

No. Meeting the minimum UTME score and other admission requirements does not guarantee admission. Final decisions are subject to the university's screening and selection process.

Q13. Can I change my selected programme?

The procedure for changing a selected programme has not been provided in the current sample university information. Please contact the admissions office.

4. Programme Enquiries

Q14. Does Westbridge University offer Computer Science?

Yes. Computer Science is offered under the Faculty of Computing and Technology. The sample programme duration is four years.

Q15. What is the difference between Computer Science and Software Engineering?

Computer Science focuses broadly on computing principles, algorithms, programming and computer systems. Software Engineering focuses more specifically on designing, developing, testing and maintaining software applications and systems.

Q16. Does Westbridge University offer Mass Communication?

Yes. Mass Communication is offered under the Faculty of Communication and Humanities. Its sample programme duration is four years.

Q17. Does Westbridge University offer Biochemistry?

Yes. Biochemistry is offered under the Faculty of Science. Its sample programme duration is four years.

Q18. Can I ask about a programme that is not listed?

Yes. However, the assistant can only confirm programmes included in the current sample catalogue. If a programme is not listed, the assistant should explain that it is not currently included in the catalogue and direct the applicant to the admissions office.

9. Instructions for AdmitEase AI

When responding to frequently asked questions, AdmitEase AI must:

Use the approved answers in this document as its primary FAQ reference.

Keep responses clear, friendly and concise.

Adapt answers naturally to the applicant's wording rather than repeating responses mechanically.

Provide additional explanations when an applicant needs clarification.

Avoid contradicting the University Profile, Programme Catalogue, Admission Requirements, Application Guide or Fees and Deadlines documents.

Never invent university details, programmes, fees, dates, contact information or application links.

Clearly state when information is unavailable.

Refer applicants to the admissions office for enquiries that require official confirmation or personal assistance.

Never guarantee admission or claim access to private applicant records.
