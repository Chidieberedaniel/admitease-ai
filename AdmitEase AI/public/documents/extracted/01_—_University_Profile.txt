1. Institutional Overview

Westbridge University is a fictional private university located in Lagos State, Nigeria.

The institution is established around the belief that quality education should develop not only academic knowledge but also practical skills, personal character, creativity and responsible leadership.

Westbridge University provides undergraduate academic programmes across four faculties, creating opportunities for students to develop knowledge and competencies relevant to their chosen fields.

The university's institutional identity is built around three principles: Knowledge, Character and Opportunity.

2. Vision Statement

To become a recognised centre of academic excellence, innovation and responsible leadership, producing graduates equipped to contribute meaningfully to society.

3. Mission Statement

To provide quality, student-centred education that develops knowledgeable, ethical and innovative graduates through academic excellence, practical learning and personal development.

4. Core Values

Westbridge University is guided by the following values:

Academic Excellence: Encouraging high academic standards, curiosity and continuous learning.

Integrity: Promoting honesty, accountability and ethical conduct.

Innovation: Encouraging creativity, critical thinking and problem-solving.

Inclusiveness: Creating a respectful and supportive learning environment.

Leadership: Developing responsible individuals who can positively influence their communities.

Service: Encouraging students to contribute meaningfully to society.
